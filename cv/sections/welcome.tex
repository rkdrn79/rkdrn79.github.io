\section{Research Interests}

\begin{onecolentry}
    I am an M.S./Ph.D. student at the Visual Information Processing Lab, Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Joonseok Lee. My research focuses on video understanding, especially temporal dynamics and high-level semantics in long-form videos. I build reliable multimodal representations for video summarization, retrieval, and motion understanding.
