\section{Research Interests}

\begin{onecolentry}
    I am an M.S./Ph.D. student at the Visual Information Processing Lab, Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Joonseok Lee.
    My research focuses on video understanding, with an emphasis on modeling temporal dynamics and high-level semantics in long-form videos. I am especially interested in building reliable multimodal representations for downstream tasks such as video summarization, video retrieval, and motion-related understanding.
\end{onecolentry}
