\section{Research Interests}

\begin{onecolentry}
    I am an M.S./Ph.D. student at the Visual Information Processing Lab, Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Joonseok Lee. My research centers on video understanding and world models. I study multimodal representations that capture temporal dynamics, high-level semantics, and physical interactions, with applications to long-form video understanding, summarization, retrieval, motion understanding, and planning.
\end{onecolentry}
